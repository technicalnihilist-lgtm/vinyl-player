Vinyl Player

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026 · applies to Vinyl Player com.hubclue.vinylplayer

The short version

Vinyl Player collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to.

The app is built without the INTERNET permission. It cannot open a network connection even if it wanted to — Android will not allow it. Everything the app does happens on your own device.

What the app collects

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No account, and no sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone.
  • No device identifiers are read or stored.

The one permission it asks for

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (on Android 13 and newer), or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (on Android 12 and older, limited to that use).

This is what lets the app show the music already on your phone. It is read-only: the app never edits, moves, or deletes a file. Your songs, and the cover art inside them, are read on the device and are never copied anywhere.

If you deny it, the app simply shows an empty library. Nothing else changes.

What stays on your device

Your settings (which switches you turned on) and your liked tracks are stored in the app's own local storage on your device. They are never uploaded, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing leaves the device. There are no third-party SDKs in the app — no analytics, no ad networks, no crash reporters.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Since the app cannot talk to a network, any change would have to arrive as an app update you choose to install.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
