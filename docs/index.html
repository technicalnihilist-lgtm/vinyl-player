Installing on Android

It is a normal Android app. Sideload it and you are done.

  1. Download the APK Tap the download button and save vinyl-player.apk to your phone.
  2. Allow installing from this source Android will ask once — open Settings and turn on "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.
  3. Open it and allow music access The app asks for your music library on first launch. Say yes and your songs appear.
