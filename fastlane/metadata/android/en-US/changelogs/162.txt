Ten fixes and speed-ups for this release.
Fixed: adding the same folder twice no longer adds every song a second time.
Fixed: one unreadable library scan no longer empties the playlist or stops the music.
Fixed: dragging the playlist no longer stops when the pointer leaves the list.
Fixed: the settings sheet takes the keyboard focus and gives it back when it closes.
Liking a song, playing or pausing rebuilds one row instead of the whole library.
A rescan builds no URLs and hashes nothing for songs that have not changed.
The highlight behind the playing row is read from the list instead of searched for.
The release build now fails if a test fails, and the stylesheet is smaller.
